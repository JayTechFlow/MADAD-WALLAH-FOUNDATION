Refund & Cancellation Policy

For donations and financial contributions · Last updated: August 2026

Home / Refund Policy

This policy outlines the principles governing donations made to MADAD WALLAH FOUNDATION through online payments, bank transfers, or UPI.

1. Voluntary Contributions

Donations made to MADAD WALLAH FOUNDATION are voluntary charitable contributions dedicated to non-profit social welfare programmes under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013. In ordinary circumstances, donations are non-refundable once receipted.

2. Circumstances for Refund Review

  • Accidental Duplicate Deduction: If your account is debited twice for a single intended contribution due to network or gateway glitch.
  • Erroneous Transfer: A demonstrated technical error where funds were transferred incorrectly.
  • Unauthorised / Disputed Transaction: Legitimate bank-verified claims of fraud or unauthorised access.

3. Refund Request Process

  1. Send an email to madadwallahfoundation@gmail.com within 7 days of transaction.
  2. Provide transaction reference (UTR / Order ID), payment screenshot, date, amount, and donor contact details.
  3. Our accounts team will verify transaction records and reply within 3 working days.

4. Timelines

Verified and approved refunds are credited back to the original source within 7–14 working days, per standard banking clearing cycles.
