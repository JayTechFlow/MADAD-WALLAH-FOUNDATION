Memorandum of Association

Six Domains of Service

Every programme we run falls within these six objective domains registered under Section 8 of the Companies Act, 2013.

📚

Education & Skill Development

  • Promoting literacy and educational aid
  • Youth empowerment programmes
  • Vocational & skill training sessions
  • Support for underprivileged students
🏥

Health & Family Welfare

  • Organising free medical check-up camps
  • Hygiene & sanitation drives
  • Health & disease awareness programmes
  • Essential medicines & care distribution
👩‍👧

Women & Child Empowerment

  • Child education & welfare support
  • Women safety & rights awareness
  • Livelihood & self-reliance workshops
  • Care and dignity for the elderly
⚖️

Social Welfare & Legal Rights

  • Community upliftment programmes
  • Human rights & civic awareness
  • Legal assistance & guidance
  • Poverty alleviation initiatives
🌳

Environment & Water Management

  • Tree plantation & green cover drives
  • Environmental conservation awareness
  • Safe drinking water access initiatives
  • Rural sanitation improvement
⛑️

Disaster Relief & Aid

  • Emergency food & ration distribution
  • Warm clothing & essentials assistance
  • Shelter support during calamities
  • Rapid response coordination
