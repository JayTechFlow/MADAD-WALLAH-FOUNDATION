Privacy Policy

Home / Privacy Policy

MADAD WALLAH FOUNDATION ("we", "our", "the Foundation") respects and protects your privacy. This policy outlines how we handle and protect personal information collected through this website.

1. Information We Collect

  • Volunteer / Membership details: When you submit our registration form: full name, mobile number, email address, birthdate celebration date, blood group, bodily identification mark, state, district, educational qualification, role applied for, and preferred sectors of interest.
  • Donation information: Information shared when making a financial contribution (donor name, contact details, payment transaction reference). Bank transfers and UPI transactions are processed through official banking channels; we do not store private payment credentials.
  • Correspondence: Messages sent via our contact form, email or telephone helpline.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To coordinate volunteer drives, member mobilization, and regional outreach.
  • To issue formal donation receipts and 80G tax exemption certificates.
  • To meet regulatory reporting requirements under the Companies Act, 2013 and applicable Indian law.
  • To communicate periodic updates and welfare drive reports.

3. Data Protection & Confidentiality

We do not sell, rent or monetize your personal data. Information is retained strictly for statutory recordkeeping and programme operations, secured using appropriate safeguards.

4. Your Rights

You may request review, correction, or removal of your personal contact records by emailing us at madadwallahfoundation@gmail.com.
